## Findings

### 1. Mixed taxonomy under Solutions

`Solutions` contains:

- Kubernetes — technology/domain context;
- Enterprise — audience;
- Start Here — lifecycle step.

These items answer different user questions and make the category unpredictable.

### 2. Conversion path is structurally split

`Contact` is under `About`, while the primary business goal is qualified technical demo intent.

This mixes company communication with product evaluation.

### 3. Homepage repeats the same taxonomy problem

Kubernetes and Enterprise appear as peer sections even though they represent different organizing models.

### 4. Product structure is comparatively coherent

`Observe` and `Automate` are both product capabilities and can remain grouped under Product/Platform.

### 5. Resource grouping is mostly coherent

Blog and Docs are both resource/content destinations, although they serve different depths of intent.

## Content integrity baseline

All required content from the input must receive an explicit destination during redesign.
