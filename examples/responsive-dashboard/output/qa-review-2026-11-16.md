## Product decisions / explicit non-goals

1. **Single-screen at 1366 × 768 is not achieved** (recorded full-page height: 2722px). Full text, evidence and supporting charts are preserved instead of silently hiding content or shrinking it to unreadable sizes.
2. AI observations remain the primary task; a mobile task-first shortcut mitigates delayed visibility of the full list.
3. The optional v0.2 specimen does not alter v0.1 official Benchmark cases, scoring, release policy or `SKILL.md`.
4. No actual data source, authentication, backend persistence or audit trail exists in the synthetic specimen.
