01 / WORKFLOW

從最不穩定的環節開始。Start at the earliest unstable layer.

每個階段都留下可接續的規格與判斷，讓下一位協作者有依據可循。Preserve the specification and evidence at each stage so another contributor can continue.

  1. 01—02

    探索與組織Discover & structure

    整理目標、現況與內容，再調整分類及導覽。

    Audit goals, evidence and content. Define information architecture and navigation.

  2. 03—04

    線框與視覺方向Wireframe & explore

    先固定資訊層級，再比較適合專案的視覺方向。

    Define hierarchy and content flow before comparing visual directions.

  3. 05—06

    系統與實作Systemize & implement

    沿用專案設計 token、元件與資料所有權。

    Use the project's design tokens, reusable components and data ownership.

  4. 07

    驗收與交接Validate & hand off

    檢查內容、互動、響應式行為與可及性，記錄限制。

    Check content, interaction, responsive behavior and accessibility. Record limits.
